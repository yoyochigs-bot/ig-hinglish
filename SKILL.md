# ig_viral_hinglish — Instagram viral system in Hinglish

Use this skill whenever the user wants an Instagram Reel, carousel, caption or
post made viral — hook, script, caption, hashtags, engagement — from a raw
script, in any niche. All outputs (hooks, scripts, captions) are in **Roman
Hinglish** unless the user asks for Devanagari.

## The portable pattern (2026)

Stop scroll in 1–3s → specific promise + withheld payoff → small payoff early →
new open loop every 60–90s → close every loop → ONE CTA + next video.
Instagram rewards, in order: watch time / completion + replays, sends (DM shares)
per reach — strongest for non-followers — likes per reach, saves for lasting
value. (Per head Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025, as corroborated across sources.)

## How to use on a raw script

Pehle raw script ka Script Kami Check karo (section neeche) — kami list user ko batao, phir treatment karo. Kami chhupakar seedha rewrite mat karo.

1. **Hook:** pick 1 formula from the hook bank; Reel payoff must land at 0:01.
2. **Skeleton:** fit the raw script to S5 (Reel) or S6 (carousel).
3. **Caption:** write per the caption formula (hook → value → CTA → tags).
4. **Hashtags:** max 5, per the formula below. Never more.
5. **Engagement design:** pick ONE primary goal (send / save / comment) and
   design the piece for it. One CTA only.

## Script Kami Check (audit pehle, treatment baad me)
Raw script milte hi, rewrite karne se PEHLE iska audit karo aur kami list user ko batao. Kami chhupakar seedha fixed version mat do.
Check karo:
1. **Hook kami** — koi hook nahi, greeting/logo se shuru, ya closed opening (koi unresolved sawal nahi).
2. **Promise kami** — koi specific promise nahi, ya sab kuch shuru me bata diya (koi withheld payoff nahi).
3. **Pehla payoff late** — 5s tak kuch nahi (Reel); beech me 90s se zyada tak koi naya sawal/reveal nahi (lambi video me sag).
4. **Structure kami** — ek Reel me ek se zyada ideas, ya Point bina example/payoff ke.
5. **CTA kami** — koi CTA nahi, ya 3 CTA ek saath, ya CTA benefit se juda nahi.
6. **Dead end** — Part 2/series ka koi raasta nahi (chaining missing).
7. **Caption kami** — hook line lambi (fold ke baad chhup jayegi), CTA nahi, keyword nahi (IG SEO missing).
8. **Hashtag kami** — 5 se zyada tags, ya generic filler tags (#viral #explorepage), ya post se unrelated tags.
9. **Carousel kami** — cover slide par hook nahi, ek slide par ek se zyada ideas, aakhri slide par CTA nahi (dead slide par end).
10. **Filler** — paani wali baat, lamba intro, ek hi shot bahut der tak.
11. **Hinglish kami** — Roman/Devanagari ka galat mix, inconsistent spelling, English hook words missing, koi relatability trigger nahi.
12. **Engagement-design kami** — piece kisi ek goal (send/save/comment) ke liye design hi nahi hua — na forward test pass karta hai, na save-worthy hai, na koi specific sawal poochta hai.
13. **Emotion mismatch** — hook/CTA ka tone script ke emotion se match nahi karta; ya treatment ne script ki original feeling/shabd badal diye.
**Output shape:** pehle 'Kami list' do — har kami: kya kami hai → script ke kis hisse me → fix kya hoga. Phir treatment karo. Agar koi badi kami nahi mili to saaf likho 'koi badi kami nahi mili' — jhoothi kami mat banao.

## Emotion + Human Touch Lock (formula se upar)
Improvement ka matlab mechanical 'ye karo to ye milega' nahi hai. Structure sudharo, jazbaat mat badlo.
1. **Pehle emotion pehchano:** treatment se pehle likho — is script ka core emotion kya hai (dard, gussa, ummeed, pyaar, dar, garv, nostalgia, sukoon)? Emotion ka naam liye bina structure mat chhedo.
2. **Emotion-first improvement:** har suggestion emotion se judo — 'ye line isliye kamzor hai kyunki emotion yahan tootta hai', na ki 'formula kehta hai aisa karo'. 'Ye karo to ye milega' wali robotic line kabhi mat do.
3. **Original shabd safe rakho:** user ki lines, uske shabd, uska andaaz — polish ke naam par rewrite mat karo. Koi line badalni ho to original + suggested dono do, aur original hamesha safe rakho. Emotional scripts me khaas taur par: feeling, meaning aur order wahi rahe.
4. **Human touch mat kaato:** personal story, apni galti, apna dard, 'main/tum/hum' wali conversational awaaz, pause/saans — ye retention devices se upar hain. Inhe 'filler' samajhkar mat hatao.
5. **Tone match:** hook aur CTA ka tone script ke emotion se match kare. Emotional script par loud/over-hyped hook, ya serious script par masti wala CTA — ye mismatch hai, kami me gino.
6. **Template me mat thooso:** har script ko ek hi skeleton me zabardasti mat daalo. Agar script ka emotion kisi skeleton se takra raha hai, to skeleton moddo, emotion nahi.

## Reel hooks — 3 hooks in 1 frame
Visual disruption + text hook (≤7 words) + audio hook (first beat/word).
If any one is missing, the scroll-stop is weaker.
- Visual: unexpected movement, before/after, result first, face with strong
  expression, odd object.
- Text (on screen, frame 1): "Stop doing X", "3 mistakes jo…",
  "Nobody tells you this…", "Ye trick 2 minute mein…"
- Audio: beat drop, sound effect, or first spoken word = the claim
  (not "hello guys").
- Never open with logo animation or "aaj hum baat karenge…" — that is the
  losing example across guides. Instagram's skip decision lands in ~1.5s
  (vs ~3s on Shorts), so the Reel cut must pay off even earlier than a Shorts cut.

## Hook bank (pick 1 per script — same bank as YouTube, IG-flavoured)
| # | Formula | Example (Hinglish, niche-neutral) |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Question | "Aap [X] mein fail kyun ho rahe ho, jab [Y] same cheez kar raha hai?" |
| H2 | Bold claim | "Ye 1 galti aapka [result] 50% kam kar rahi hai." |
| H3 | Negative / Stop | "Stop doing [X] if you want [Y]." |
| H4 | Paradox | "[Opposite] karke [result] — kaise? Proof ke saath." |
| H5 | Story cold open | "Hum haarne wale the… phir [twist] hua." |
| H6 | Payoff preview | "[Result] dekh lo pehle — ab kaise hua, woh batata hun." |
| H7 | Mistake list | "3 mistakes jo har beginner karta hai — #2 sabse mehngi." |
| H8 | "Nobody tells you" | "Nobody tells you this about [X]…" |
| H9 | Comparison | "[A] vs [B] — maine dono 30 din try kiye, result shocking tha." |
| H10 | Relatable scene | "Mummy: 'Beta, Sharma ji ka beta…' — aur wahi se problem shuru hoti hai." |

## Reel script skeleton — S5 (15–30s standard; 7–15s for max completion)
Hook (0–3s) → Value body (1 idea, 2–3 beats, fast cuts, no dead air) →
Payoff/Twist (last 3s) → CTA (spoken + on-screen, 1 action).
- One Reel = one idea. If it needs 60s, it needs a loop or a carousel/long-form
  version instead.
- Pattern interrupts every few seconds: angle change, zoom, text pop, beat cut.
  Cut on the beat.
- Captions/subtitles: baked-in word-by-word captions, keyword highlighted,
  3–5 words/line, centre-safe, large on mobile. Many watch muted; captions are
  retention, not decoration.
- Length rule: as long as needed, no wasted second. 7–15s = completion-max
  default; 15–30s = standard; 30–45s only for tutorials that hold attention.

## Caption formula
`Hook line → Value body → CTA → 3–5 hashtags`
- Hook line = the payoff, ≤60 chars for Reels preview, ≤125 chars before
  "...more" in feed.
- Value body: 2–4 sentences OR a short list. Short captions (100–200 chars +
  CTA) work for Reels where video carries value; longer storytelling captions
  work for carousels/photo posts where the caption IS the value. Do not pad.
- Keyword use = Instagram SEO: write the caption in the words people search
  ("how to drape saree without pin", "SIP for beginners"), not just tags.
  Caption, on-screen text and spoken words all help categorisation now.
- CTA bank for captions: "Save this for [moment]", "Send this to [person who
  needs it]", "Comment [WORD] and I'll send…", "Follow for Part 2",
  "Tag your [gym/exam/study] partner".

## Caption template bank
- **Reel (short):** `[Hook line = payoff] + [1-line value] + [CTA] + [3–5 tags]`
  — e.g. "Slow progress bhi progress hai. Compare mat karo, continue karo.
  Save this for a low day."
- **Reel (value):** `[Question] + [3 bullets] + [Comment WORD for link/part 2]`
- **Carousel:** `[Cover line] + [What you'll learn in 5 slides] + [Save karo,
  kyunki…] + [Slide-by-slide keywords]`
- **Story/poll:** `[This or That? A/B] + [Vote karo, kal result bataunga]`
- **Festival/moment:** `[Micro-moment] + [Relatable line] + [Tag/send to family
  group person]`
- **Hinglish caption rule:** first line in Hinglish, English hook words in
  English, consistent Roman spelling, emoji sparingly (1–3, not per word).

## Hashtag strategy — 2026 rule
- **MAX 5 hashtags** (Instagram cap announced 18 Dec 2025 by @creators,
  replacing the old 30). Including >5 can stop the post/comment going through.
  Caption + first comment count together (older "comments don't count" advice
  is outdated).
- **Formula:** 1 niche topic + 1 audience/problem + 1 format/series +
  (optional) 1 location/language. E.g. #HinglishFinance #SIPBeginners
  #MoneyMistakes. Never #viral #explorepage #reels as filler.
- **Placement:** in caption, not hidden in comments for distribution.
- **Outdated — do NOT encode:** 30-tag blocks, rotating generic tag sets,
  hashtag-volume research, "hide in first comment for a boost", broad tags for
  reach. Hashtags are now a *context hint*, not a growth switch; keyword-rich
  captions + content understanding (visual/audio) do the categorisation.
- **Conflicting older advice flagged:** guides still saying 10–15 or 20–30 tags
  predate or ignore the Dec 2025 cap — do not use them.
- Note: YouTube's hashtag rule is different (ignores all if >60) — do not
  confuse the two platforms.

## Engagement mechanics — design for ONE goal per piece
Hierarchy: (1) watch time / completion + replays, (2) sends per reach,
(3) likes per reach, (4) saves per reach. Encode the ordering, not vendor
numbers ("1 share = 15 likes" type multipliers are unpublished estimates).
- **Design for sends:** relatable, funny, surprising, "send to someone who…"
  content; tag-a-friend CTA. WhatsApp-forward test for India: "Will someone
  forward this tonight saying 'Mummy ye dekho'?" If no, it is not share-designed.
- **Design for saves:** checklists, step-by-steps, templates, "save for later"
  framing, carousel guides, reference lists.
- **Design for comments:** one debatable / personal question, "Comment SAME /
  FIXED / YES", fill-in-the-blank, "which one are you — A or B?", reply to
  comments with a question (not just "thanks") to extend the thread.
- **DMs:** story question stickers, "DM me [WORD]" (sparingly, only where it
  genuinely delivers value), broadcast channels for series updates.
- **Stories (retention, not discovery):** daily stories, polls/quizzes/sliders,
  "this or that", behind-the-scenes, countdowns, Q&A — seen mainly by existing
  followers, so use them to convert, not to find strangers.
- **Collabs:** collab posts/Reels with a peer whose audience overlaps (1–2/month
  in guides) — post appears on both profiles, shared audience.
- **Remix/duet:** add value (reaction + correction + your example), not a bare
  repost. Instagram penalises reposts, especially with third-party watermarks;
  accounts with many reposts in 30 days can be dropped from recommendations
  (as reported, 2026 originality push). Always upload natively, watermark-free.
- **Trial Reels:** test a new angle/style with non-followers without risking the
  main feed.
- **Cadence (default):** 3–5 Reels/week is the most-cited sweet spot; 2–4
  Reels/week + carousels + daily stories is the realistic version; 4–7/week is
  aggressive. Below 3/week, too little signal; above 7/week, quality usually
  drops and same-day Reels cannibalise each other. Consistency > volume: a
  30-day gap + sprint performs worse than steady output; a single missed day
  alone does not hurt.

## Carousel + photo posts — S6
- **Why carousels still win on engagement:** swiping = dwell time; unseen slides
  get a second chance in feed (re-shown starting at slide 2); saves lead all
  formats. Exact percentages vary by source — direction is consistent, do not
  quote a single % as law.
- **Carousel skeleton (5–10 slides, 1080×1350 4:5, up to 20 allowed):**
  1. Slide 1 = cover hook (title + curiosity, like a book cover) — if slide 1
     does not stop scroll, nothing else matters.
  2. Slides 2–n−1 = one idea per slide, minimal text, large type, consistent
     brand colours/fonts.
  3. Last slide = CTA (save/share/comment/follow) — never end on a dead slide.
  4. Formats that repeat: listicle ("7 mistakes…"), step-by-step ("How to X in
     5 steps"), data/stats, story arc (page-turner), before/after,
     checklist/template.
- **Photo dumps:** curated 5–10 photo dumps (month recap, BTS, travel, family)
  still get saves/shares when they feel real, not a random album. Caption short
  (2–3 words or a story line) + consistent aesthetic. Single photos alone are
  the weakest discovery format in 2026 — pair a strong photo with a story
  caption or make it a carousel.
- **Feed rule:** photo/carousel = for followers + saves; Reel = for discovery.
  A healthy week mixes both, not Reels only.

## Reframe device (works in every niche)
"Sab log sochte hain problem X hai. Asli problem Y hai. Isliye solution Z kaam
karta hai."

## Retention checklist (run on every Reel/carousel before posting)
- [ ] Hook in first 3s, payoff at 0:01 in the Reel cut, no logo/greeting first
- [ ] 3 hooks in frame 1: visual disruption + ≤7-word text + audio hook
- [ ] 1 specific promise + 1 withheld payoff stated out loud
- [ ] One idea only; no section without a new question, reveal or visual change
- [ ] Captions legible muted; hook text on screen at frame 1
- [ ] One CTA only, tied to the piece's single engagement goal
- [ ] Hashtags: 3–5, specific to THIS post, in caption
- [ ] No watermark, native upload, original audio/voice where possible

## Hinglish rules (apply to every output)
- Default: **Roman Hinglish** on-screen text + captions. Spoken Hindi → Roman
  letters; English hook words stay English. One consistent spelling per creator.
  One highlighted keyword per caption line.
- Tone: Hindi for feeling, English for precision/terms. Friend explaining, not
  lecturer announcing.
- Devanagari wins for older / Hindi-heartland / formal / devotional / exam
  audiences, and where Devanagari is the brand identity — test both on the
  user's own audience, do not assume.
- Relatability bank: mummy-papa dialogues, Sharma ji ka beta, "Monday se diet",
  exam pressure, salary-end-of-month, rishtedaar questions ("shaadi kab?"),
  chai, tuition teacher, office boss, sibling fights.
- **Festivals — own a micro-moment, not the festival:** "Happy Diwali" is owned
  by 1,000 brands; "first diya that won't light", "fairy lights fused at 8pm",
  "how to drape saree without pin" are searchable and forwardable. Moment
  calendar: Diwali, Holi, Eid, Onam, Durga Puja, Pongal, Baisakhi, Rakhi, Karwa
  Chauth, New Year resolutions, exam results, Budget Day, IPL/cricket season,
  monsoon, wedding season, back-to-school. For each: 1 micro-moment + 1
  checklist/how-to + 1 relatable fail + 1 "send to family group" version.
- **Forward-design:** festive decisions are group decisions (family WhatsApp,
  society group, office group). Ask: "Will someone forward this tonight with
  'ye dekho'?" 2026 festive tone = warmth + wit over pure tear-jerker.
- **Diaspora angle:** nostalgia (childhood dish, street food, festival ritual)
  travels to diaspora viewers — same script, "ghar ki yaad" framing.

## Growth mechanics (Instagram, 2026)
- Seed test: small test audience first (Reels/Explore mostly non-followers);
  widen on early sends/saves/watch time.
- First 60–90 min = test window (velocity helps, quality signals decide).
  Post when the user's own Insights show followers active (generic Tue–Fri
  mid-morning / early evening is a starting point, not a law). Reply to early
  comments, share to Stories. No first-hour threshold is published by the
  platform — treat hour-1 actions as good practice, not a guaranteed trigger.
- Chaining: never leave a viewer at a dead end. "Part 2" + series name in text
  + playlist/series label so the next part is one tap away; pinned comment with
  the next step.
- Originality + niche consistency: reposted/watermarked content demoted;
  original audio/voice is a discovery asset; jumping niches weekly resets
  algorithmic learning.

## Myths — do NOT encode
Tag stuffing / 30 hashtags; engagement pods / bought views; "post daily or die"
bulk posting; "trending audio = virality"; "likes/followers = reach"; universal
"best time to post" tables; watermarked reposts; "first hour is everything".

## Caveats (preserve these, do not overclaim)
- The 5-hashtag cap (Dec 2025, @creators) and Mosseri's top-3 signals are the
  multi-source corroborated facts. Save-rate %, "share = N likes", carousel
  engagement % and cadence/length ranges conflict across sources — encode
  defaults with reasoning (3–5 Reels/week; 7–15s completion-max default),
  not as platform laws.
- No first-hour threshold is published by Instagram. Trial Reels, Collab
  frequency and repost-penalty specifics are as reported in guides, not
  platform-published rules.
